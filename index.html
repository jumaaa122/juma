Profile

A career built on trust,
governance and quiet authority.

Gerald Juma is a Senior Insurance & Risk Advisory Executive with over 30 years’ experience delivering insurance and governance solutions across public sector institutions, metropolitan municipalities and parastatal organisations. His work has shaped risk strategy for some of South Africa’s most consequential institutional relationships — including the City of Johannesburg Metropolitan Municipality and Telkom — specialising in municipal insurance programmes, regulatory compliance and large-scale risk management within complex public sector environments.

Today, as Director of First 23rd Group (Pty) Ltd, he leads an authorised Financial Services Provider delivering specialised insurance and advisory solutions to corporate and institutional clients — designing programmes aligned to regulatory and governance requirements, and structuring strategic risk advisory across regulated sectors.
